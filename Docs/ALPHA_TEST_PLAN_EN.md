# CALM FACES — Alpha test plan

Thank you for testing CALM FACES. This is an **early alpha**: things will break, and that is the point.
Your job is to try **everything**, break what you can, and tell us exactly what happened.

> **Please keep it private.** No videos, screenshots or streams outside the testers' channel until we say so.
> **Content warning:** horror, jumpscares, loud screams, red flashes (can be turned off), dark spaces.

---

## 0. Before you start

- [ ] 0.1 Install CALM FACES from your Quest library (it comes from the **ALPHA** channel invite).
- [ ] 0.2 Set a **roomscale Guardian** with space to swing your arms. You move by pushing the world with your hands.
- [ ] 0.3 Turn your headset volume up to a normal level (voice and sound matter in this game).
- [ ] 0.4 Learn to record a clip: Meta button → Camera → Record video. **A 20-second clip of a bug is worth 100 words.**
- [ ] 0.5 Note your headset model (Quest 2 / 3 / 3S / Pro) and include it in every report.

**How to report** (one message per bug, in the testers' Discord channel):
```
ID: (test number, e.g. 6.3)
Headset: Quest 3
What happened:
What I expected:
Where (room / near what):
How often: always / sometimes / once
Clip or screenshot: (attach)
```
Also report things that feel **bad** even if they are not bugs: too dark, too hard, boring, confusing, nauseating.

## 1. First launch and tutorial

- [ ] 1.1 The game launches without crashing; loading time feels acceptable (note roughly how long).
- [ ] 1.2 You spawn in the **Playroom** (warm light, safe zone).
- [ ] 1.3 A floating tutorial panel appears. It advances only when you actually move ~2.5 m with your hands, then jump.
- [ ] 1.4 The tutorial texts are readable (size, distance, contrast). Note any text that is hard to read.
- [ ] 1.5 The tutorial explains "the voice" and "the safe room" clearly. Did you understand what to do next?
- [ ] 1.6 On the COMFORT panel, the **TUTO** button restarts the tutorial, and skips it if it is running.
- [ ] 1.7 You have no legs (by design). Hands and fingers follow your controllers; grip/trigger close the fingers.

## 2. Movement and physics (most important section)

You move like in arm-locomotion games: push the floor or walls with your hands.

- [ ] 2.1 Walking by pushing the floor feels responsive, not floaty, not jittery.
- [ ] 2.2 Jumping (push down hard and release) gives a satisfying leap. Try long jumps across gaps.
- [ ] 2.3 Climbing: grab/push walls, furniture, beds, crates, pipes. You should be able to climb most things.
- [ ] 2.4 Wall-running / sliding along walls feels fluid (a little slide is intended).
- [ ] 2.5 Hands never get stuck inside walls or objects. If they do, note exactly where.
- [ ] 2.6 You never fall through the floor, and never get launched into the air for no reason.
- [ ] 2.7 Try to clip through walls, doors, windows and the ceiling by pushing hard. Report anything that lets you out.
- [ ] 2.8 If you fall out of the map, you are brought back to spawn.
- [ ] 2.9 **COMFORT panel → MOVES**: switch between **FAST** (default) and **CLASSIC**. Play 5 minutes with each.
      Which one feels better? Which one is closer to the arm-locomotion games you know?
- [ ] 2.10 The "Test dummy" in the Playroom has a player-sized body: climb on its head.
- [ ] 2.11 Any motion sickness? When exactly (sliding, falling, jumping, being launched)?
- [ ] 2.12 Footstep sounds change with the surface (wood, tiles, metal, carpet).

## 3. The Playroom (safe zone)

- [ ] 3.1 Monsters never enter the Playroom and never catch you inside it.
- [ ] 3.2 **Crayon box** (yellow box): press the pink button → +100 crayons, coin sound, then a `NEXT IN hh:mm:ss` timer (7 h).
- [ ] 3.3 You slowly earn crayons while playing outside the safe zone. The crayon counter updates.
- [ ] 3.4 **Shop**: browse the cosmetics, buy one you can afford, equip it, unequip it. A cosmetic you cannot afford must not be bought.
- [ ] 3.5 Equipped cosmetics are visible on your doll (look in the mirror / ask another player in multiplayer).
- [ ] 3.6 **Objectives board**: PAPERS x/N and PLACES x/N update as you find them.
- [ ] 3.7 **COMFORT panel**: FLASH (red flashes ON/OFF), SCARE (FULL / SOFT), MOVES (FAST / CLASSIC), TUTO. Each press changes the text and is remembered after restarting the game.
- [ ] 3.8 **Mode wall** ("CHOOSE YOUR GAME"): EXPLORATION works. INK TAG and HIDE & SEEK are marked as coming soon and cannot be started.
- [ ] 3.9 **PLAYERS board**: solo shows "Nobody else here." and a line `YOUR ID: XXXXXXXX`. Send us your ID.
- [ ] 3.10 **Minitel** (old terminal on the small desk, keys ◄ ► OK BACK):
  - [ ] change your nickname;
  - [ ] change your doll colour;
  - [ ] microphone: OPEN / PUSH TO TALK (hold A or X) / MUTED;
  - [ ] ROOM page (multiplayer code, see section 9).
- [ ] 3.11 **Lore journal** in the Playroom counts the papers you found.
- [ ] 3.12 **Music box**: pick it up, carry it out of the Playroom, throw it. Once it stops, it plays ~18 s and lures the blind monster to it. It respawns later.

## 4. Grabbing, throwing, buttons

- [ ] 4.1 Grab papers, balls, music boxes; throw them. Throws go where you expect.
- [ ] 4.2 Papers are readable when you bring them close to your face.
- [ ] 4.3 Every pink button reacts to a press (doors, lifts, tapes, projector, bell, crayon box, comfort panel).
- [ ] 4.4 Buttons do not trigger by accident when you just walk past them.
- [ ] 4.5 Dropped or thrown objects never fall through the floor (if they do, note where).

## 5. Voice and noise (your microphone matters)

- [ ] 5.1 Your doll's mouth moves when you talk (see it in the mirror).
- [ ] 5.2 Whispering: monsters do not react.
- [ ] 5.3 Normal talking: the blind monster (L'Écoute) reacts if she is close (~5 m).
- [ ] 5.4 Shouting: she hears you from far away (~30 m) and hunts you; the Doctor comes running.
- [ ] 5.5 Minitel → MUTED: monsters no longer hear your voice at all.
- [ ] 5.6 Loud footsteps on metal carry further than on carpet.

## 6. Exploring the Institute

Find every place (a title appears in front of your eyes the first time) and every paper. Check each area:

- [ ] 6.1 Hall / reception.
- [ ] 6.2 Corridors with **button doors**: a pink button next to a door closes / opens it (and makes noise).
- [ ] 6.3 **Big dormitory**: dolls sitting on beds turn their heads when you look away; a drawing on the wall changes when you look away for a few seconds.
- [ ] 6.4 **Switchboard (Standard)**: the phone rings at random. Pick it up while it rings: a whisper and a line of story appear (6 messages in order). The ringing attracts the blind monster.
- [ ] 6.5 **Theatre**: go through the curtain slit; balcony reachable (refuge).
- [ ] 6.6 **Dressing room** behind the stage: the mirror shows a doll behind you that is not really there; it gets closer each time you look away.
- [ ] 6.7 **Basement** loop: machine room → gallery of small doors (knocking) → fan → cistern → showers → pool. You can do the full loop.
- [ ] 6.8 **Pool**: the ink does not hold you, you sink; a ladder lets you climb out.
- [ ] 6.9 Floating chairs: they shake and fall when you approach, then come back later.
- [ ] 6.10 **Laundry** (door 23 at the end of the bedroom corridor): flooded floor, climb on machines, tables, pipes and clothes lines.
- [ ] 6.11 **Infirmary**: lift in the machine room ("INFIRMARY v"); waiting room, offices, ward, operating room, archives, cold room, isolation. Lift back up works.
- [ ] 6.12 **Attic**: lift at the back of the dormitory ("ATTIC ^"). You arrive under a safe lamp; you do not fall on arrival.
- [ ] 6.13 **Power cuts**: every few minutes the lights flicker and go out for some seconds (Playroom stays lit).
- [ ] 6.14 **Tapes** (TAPE 1 dormitory, TAPE 2 attic, TAPE 3 infirmary archives): PLAY starts the voice with subtitles; press again to stop.
- [ ] 6.15 **Slide projector** (attic): the side button shows 4 slides with captions.
- [ ] 6.16 Is the map **too dark** or **too bright** on your headset? Can you find your way?
- [ ] 6.17 Any texture, object or wall that looks broken, floating, flickering, or appears suddenly when you turn a corner.

## 7. Monsters (try to get caught by EACH of them at least once)

For each monster: is it scary? fair? too fast / too slow? does it ever get stuck, walk through walls, or catch you unfairly?

- [ ] 7.1 **L'Écoute** (blind, hears): patrols room to room, opens doors with keys. Make noise → she hunts you. Freeze and stay silent → she should lose you after a few seconds. Sitting/lying still on a dormitory bed makes you much harder to hear.
- [ ] 7.2 Refuges: on the theatre balcony she comes underneath but cannot catch you.
- [ ] 7.3 **The Softies** (theatre): they only move when nobody is looking at them.
- [ ] 7.4 **The Sheet** (laundry): touching the water or floor lets it sense you; standing on a machine keeps you safe. A thrown ball in the water distracts it.
- [ ] 7.5 **The Doctor** (infirmary): sees only what his green lantern lights, deaf to small sounds, comes for crashes and shouts. Fragile objects (trays, jars, IV stands) fall with a huge crash when you brush them. His cart squeaks; he sometimes leaves it. The reception **bell** calls him back to reception.
- [ ] 7.6 **The Night Light / La Veilleuse** (attic): fears light. Stay under a lit bulb: she waits at the edge, then the bulb buzzes and goes out. Pull the bulb's string (bead) to relight it. In the dark she charges.
- [ ] 7.7 **Jumpscares**: each monster has its own. Are they readable (you see the face) and scary? Too loud?
- [ ] 7.8 **Death**: after a scare, a few seconds of black with a whispered, subtitled line, then back to the Playroom. An ink splat stays where you died.
- [ ] 7.9 COMFORT → SCARE **SOFT** makes scares shorter and quieter. COMFORT → FLASH **OFF** removes the red flashes.
- [ ] 7.10 After catching someone, the monster pauses before leaving (it should not chain-catch you on respawn).

## 8. The ending

- [ ] 8.1 Find all papers and all places: laundry machine 23 lights up, a white mask waits there, +500 crayons (once).
- [ ] 8.2 The "CRAWL IN" button takes you to the hidden paper room; "BACK" returns you to the Playroom.
- [ ] 8.3 Was the goal clear? Did you know what to look for? How long did it take you?

## 9. Multiplayer (all testers together)

Everyone types the **same ROOM code** (4–6 digits) on the Minitel, then waits ~3 s.

- [ ] 9.1 PLAYERS board shows "PLAYERS (n)" with everyone's nickname.
- [ ] 9.2 You see the others (doll in their colour, nickname above the head, mouth moving when they talk).
- [ ] 9.3 Proximity voice: you hear others from their direction, quieter with distance, muffled behind walls.
- [ ] 9.4 Push to talk / MUTED on the Minitel work in multiplayer.
- [ ] 9.5 **MUTE** a player: you no longer hear them. **BLOCK**: they disappear and are silent; block again to unblock. **MUTE ALL** works.
- [ ] 9.6 **REPORT**: the line shows "REPORTED. THANK YOU." and the player is blocked. (Reports are not delivered to moderators yet: just check it works on your side.)
- [ ] 9.7 Monsters are in the same place for everyone; any player can be caught (not only the first one in the room).
- [ ] 9.8 When someone dies, the others see the ink splat.
- [ ] 9.9 Attic: a bulb blown out / relit by one player is the same for everyone.
- [ ] 9.10 The first player leaves: monsters keep working for the others.
- [ ] 9.11 Leave and rejoin the room; clear the code to go back to solo.
- [ ] 9.12 Climb on another player's head.
- [ ] 9.13 Lag: do other players or monsters teleport or stutter? Note your Wi-Fi quality.
- [ ] 9.14 Voice quality: echo, cuts, too quiet, too loud?

## 10. Headset and system

- [ ] 10.1 Press the Meta button during solo play: the game pauses (monsters frozen, sound off) and resumes correctly.
- [ ] 10.2 Take the headset off for a minute and put it back on: no crash, no weird state.
- [ ] 10.3 Recenter (hold Meta button): your position is correct afterwards.
- [ ] 10.4 Quit and relaunch: crayons, papers found, places, cosmetics, nickname, colour, comfort settings and blocked players are kept.
- [ ] 10.5 Performance: note any stutter, freeze or frame drop and where (dormitory, infirmary, laundry, attic are the heaviest).
- [ ] 10.6 Battery and heat after 30 minutes of play.
- [ ] 10.7 Any crash: note what you were doing just before, and send the time it happened.
