# In-game texts (English)

Tout ce qui est **visible dans le jeu** est en anglais. Les documents de travail restent en français
(voir `LORE_proposition.md` pour l'histoire complète et l'ordre des papiers).

## Lore papers, in order of discovery

### Zone 1 — Old hotel lobby

**Paper 1 — Brochure, 1974** (glossy paper, faded pastel colours)
> **Céleste's Gardens — the hotel where children laugh!**
> Come and meet the Softies, our adorable mascots! Carousel, bubble garden, and soon our
> brand-new indoor swimming pool. Here, children's laughter is our sweetest music.
> — Céleste Marivaux, creator of *The Softies*

**Paper 2 — Newspaper clipping, August 16, 1977**
> **TRAGEDY AT CÉLESTE'S GARDENS**
> A six-year-old girl, Mila Marivaux, drowned on Saturday night during the pool's opening party.
> "The music drowned out everything. Nobody heard a thing," said a waiter.
> The resort is closed until further notice.

### Zone 2 — School wing

**Paper 3 — Rules of the Marivaux Institute, 1978** (pinned to the wall)
> 1. We do not scream.
> 2. We do not run in the hallways.
> 3. We wear our calm face from waking until bedtime.
> 4. At 9 p.m., every wing's doors lock (Lock Protocol). The buttons are for staff only.
> 5. **Calm is listening to you.**

**Paper 4 — Child's note stapled to a drawing**
> Today we drew our calm face. Madam says we have to wear it until it fits.
> Mine itches. — Theo, age 8, No.3

**Paper 5 — Letter never sent** (red stamp: "NOT APPROVED — HEADMISTRESS")
> Mom, Dad,
> Lucie hasn't said a word in two weeks. But at night, the mouth on her mask moves by itself.
> I'm scared to put mine on. Please come and get me.
> Ines, No.18

### Zone 3 — Closed park

**Paper 6 — Note found in an entertainer's dressing room**
> Madam's orders: never take the Softies' heads off in front of the children.
> I haven't taken mine off in three weeks. This morning I tried. I couldn't.
> The little ones laugh when we play "Red Light, Green Light": we only move when they're not looking.
> I don't think I know how to do anything else anymore.

### Zone 4 — Pool

**Paper 7 — Lifeguard's notebook** (warped pages, ink stains)
> August 14, 1977. I didn't hear anything. I didn't hear anything. I didn't hear anything.
> …
> November 12, 1985. Madam had the pool drained. She's having it filled with ink.
> She says: "The water took her from me. The ink will give her back."

### Zone 5 — Manor (headmistress's offices)

**Paper 8 — Céleste's diary, page A**
> Nobody heard Mila. If the house had been quiet, I would have heard her.
> No child will ever scream again without me hearing it. Never again.

**Paper 9 — Céleste's diary, page B**
> The faces work. The ink keeps whatever you put into it. The children are calm, and their
> voices are safe inside the paper, where nothing can lose them.

**Paper 10 — Inspection report, October 1985**
> Visit to the Marivaux Institute. Of the 23 pupils, not one spoke a single word during the visit.
> The paper masks are worn at all times, including at meals. The headmistress refuses to let us
> remove them. We recommend the closure of the institution on **November 15, 1985**.

**Paper 11 — Night watchman's logbook**
> Night of November 13. Madam cut the power to the whole building. She ordered the Lock Protocol
> in every wing, even the pool. She said: "In the dark, you hear better."
> I'm counting the doors. 1, 2, 3… I don't know how many there are anymore.

**Paper 12 — Newspaper article, November 20, 1985**
> **MYSTERIOUS CLOSURE OF THE MARIVAUX INSTITUTE**
> 23 children and 9 staff members remain missing. Police found only drawings:
> 23 children's faces pinned to the dormitory walls. The headmistress, Céleste Marivaux,
> is also missing.

### Last paper (in the darkest area)

**Paper 13 — Child's handwriting, in pencil**
> If you're reading this, you're a face too.
> Don't talk. Don't run. Don't drop things.
> She comes to the noise. She doesn't want to hurt you, she just wants you to be quiet.
> Forever.

## Secrets

**Secret 1 — Behind a button door under the pool: Mila's last drawing.**
The only drawing made with coloured pencils, no ink. A face with its mouth wide open, and above it: "MOMMY".

**Secret 2 — A music box with a cassette tape.** Label: "Mila — birthday 1976".
Staff note tucked inside: "Madam listens to this tape every night. It's the only thing that calms her."

**Secret 3 — Staff payroll, 1985.** Nine names, including Gérard (lifeguard), Armand (night watchman)
and the park entertainers.

**Secret 4 — The 24th face.** The Face Programme register lists 24 drawings, but there were only
23 children. The 24th is a self-portrait of Céleste, its eyes rubbed out until the paper tore. On the back:
> I saw too much. I could see their eyes watching me through the paper.
> Tonight I erased mine. Since then, I hear everything.
