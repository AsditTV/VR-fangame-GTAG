## Description longue
**EN**

> The Marivaux Institute taught its 23 children to wear a calm face. One November night, the power went out,
> and none of them were ever seen again.
>
> CALM FACES is a VR horror game where you have no legs: you move by pushing the floor with your hands,
> climbing furniture and leaping across rooms. It is fast, physical and loud — and noise is exactly what gets
> you caught.
>
> **Your voice is part of the game.** Your real microphone feeds the monsters: whisper and they pass you by,
> talk and the closest ones turn their heads, scream and every one of them comes running.
>
> **Meet the staff.**
> - **The Listener** is blind. She hunts by sound alone, and charges when she is close.
> - **The Softies** only move when nobody is looking at them.
> - **The Sheet** glides through the dark corridors.
> - **The Doctor** wears a crow mask and pushes a squeaking instrument trolley through the underground
>   infirmary. If the squeaking stops, he has seen you.
>
> **Find the papers.** Thirteen pages and four secrets tell what really happened to the children — and to
> the 23rd. Explore the dormitory, the switchboard, the theatre, the ink pool, the laundry and the infirmary
> hidden beneath the boiler room.
>
> **Features**
> - Arm-based locomotion: run, jump and climb with your hands only.
> - Monsters that react to your real voice and to every object you knock over.
> - A large hand-made Institute plus an underground infirmary.
> - Collectible lore, crayons to earn and cosmetics for your doll.
> - Comfort options: turn off red flashes, soft scares mode.
> - **[MULTI]** Explore with friends in online co-op with proximity voice chat — the monsters hear them too.
>
> Content warning: jump scares, flashing lights (can be turned off), disturbing themes involving children in
> an institution (no gore).

## Confort : pourquoi « Intense »
La locomotion aux bras fait bouger la caméra vite, avec des sauts et des chutes, sans téléportation. Les joueurs
sensibles au mal des transports seront malades : mieux vaut l'annoncer que recevoir des remboursements. Confiance :
*probable* (les jeux à locomotion aux bras se déclarent en général « Intense »).
